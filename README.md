## Why Pesums

Most browsers say they protect your privacy, then phone home with sync, telemetry, and analytics. Pesums doesn't. Every block decision is made on your device, and your browsing is never collected or sent anywhere.

## Features

- **Pesums Shield** blocks ads, trackers, and malicious scripts before they load. Blocking runs on your device.
- **Keyword DNS blocking** blocks whole families of tracker domains by pattern, not just exact-match lists.
- **Anti-fingerprinting** obfuscates screen resolution, fonts, and hardware details so your device can't be uniquely identified.
- **Local password manager**: an encrypted vault with no cloud sync, protected by a master password or biometrics.
- **On-device history**: it never leaves your phone, with auto-delete and incognito mode.
- **Download manager** runs in the background with live progress, pause and resume, and clear error messages.
- **No sync, no accounts, no analytics.**

## Free trial & subscription

- **15 days free** with full access.
- After that, a **yearly subscription**. It is not a lifetime license and not a one-time purchase.
- One subscription works on **up to 3 devices**. If you sell or reset a device, log out in Pesums first to free its slot for later.

The only data Pesums uses is a device identifier for the free trial and license activation. Nothing about your browsing is collected.
